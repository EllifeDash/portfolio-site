A website is usually the first thing people check before they decide to trust you or buy from you. It needs to look sharp, answer questions fast, and not cost a fortune in data to load.

## Two flavors, depending on what you need

**Content-first sites (Astro).** Portfolios, brochure sites, blogs, and landing pages. Astro serves plain, already-rendered HTML — no heavy JavaScript waiting to boot. These routinely score 90+ on Lighthouse and open nearly instantly even on 3G.

**Interactive sites (React).** When the page needs real interactivity — complex forms, calculators, live widgets, account areas — I build the interactive pieces with React, keeping them as light as they can be.

## Built to be found on Google

Speed is a ranking factor, and so is structure. Every page gets clean semantic markup, per-page titles and descriptions, and structured data where it helps. The kind of fundamentals that make a site *easier to be found*, not harder.

## Content you can edit yourself

No proprietary editor you're tied to forever. Blog posts and pages live in simple files — you can write them yourself, keep them version-controlled, and change them in minutes without breaking anything.

## What you get

- A live site, deployed and running, with proper analytics
- Optimized for mobile — the majority of your visitors will be on phones
- No monthly theme fees or builder subscriptions to renew
- The code is yours

**Timeline:** a polished site goes live in 2–3 weeks.
