Not every job is a full system. Some of the most valuable work is small, sharp, and finished in a week — the fixes and automations that quietly save hours every single day.

## Chrome extensions

A small extension that lives in the browser and does one thing well — reporting, data entry, tracking, or bridging two tools you use constantly. Manifest V3, packaged cleanly, safe to install in an organization.

**Good for:** a team stuck copying data between systems by hand.

## Automation with n8n & GitHub Actions

n8n connects your tools — forms, sheets, email, databases — and moves data between them without anyone copying and pasting. GitHub Actions handles the developer-side automation: build, test, and deploy running themselves.

**Good for:** killing the repetitive 20-minute morning ritual.

## Supabase setup, schemas & sync

Postgres without the headache. I set up the database properly — schema, row-level security, backups — and wire the sync so mobile, web, and desktop stay in harmony.

**Good for:** apps that should share data across devices but were never connected.

## Performance & SEO audits

I audit your existing site, measure what matters (Lighthouse, Core Web Vitals, crawl-ability), and hand you a prioritized fix list — or just fix the issues for you.

**Good for:** a site that feels slow or isn't showing up in search.

## Maintenance & support

Ongoing care for a website or system: security updates, small improvements, content changes, and someone on call when something breaks.

**Good for:** owners who want their software to keep working without having to think about it.

Most of these jobs are quoted as one fixed scope and delivered within one to two weeks.
